# Vision

Updated 2026-09-17.

## The problem

Kids and parents do not lack PDFs. They lack a trusted answer to **what must get done tomorrow and this school week**, and a way to start that work without a two-hour hover.

## The product

**AI Tutor** is a family school-night OS.

1. **What is due** — one dated daily plan per child: not turned in, C or lower, tests this week, one “do first.”
2. **How they do it** — short session: steps, timer, I’m stuck, worked answer after the steps.

PowerSchool says submitted vs missing. Harvested packets are the materials. The app (and tonight, Drive Docs) is the plan.

## Who

Student (v1 grades 6–12) and parent/guardian. Not teachers or the district.

## Principles

1. Coach, don’t ghostwrite take-home work.
2. One next action.
3. Short blocks (15–25 min).
4. Parent visibility is a short factual plan, not a transcript.
5. Local household first (Joey + Reagan is the design center).
6. Spec-driven.

## Winning

A parent opens today’s Daily Plan and trusts it. The student taps Start on the first line. A second child does not require a new product.

## Not v1

Full curriculum, auto-submit to school portals, gradebook replacement, school SaaS, a per-child wiki.
